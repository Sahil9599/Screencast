Mirror Cast

Cast your mobile screen to PC — using just Hotspot!

🔒 No Internet Required
📺

PC — Receiver

Open this on your PC browser to receive and display the mobile screen stream.

URL https://{{ ip }}:{{ port }}/receiver
📱

Mobile — Sender

Open this URL in your mobile browser to share your screen to the PC.

URL https://{{ ip }}:{{ port }}/sender

⚡ Quick Setup — कैसे करें?

  1. PC पर Mobile Hotspot ON करो (Settings → Mobile Hotspot)
  2. Mobile को PC के Hotspot से Connect करो
  3. PC के browser में /receiver page खोलो
  4. Mobile के browser (Chrome) में /sender page खोलो
  5. "Start Casting" button दबाओ — screen share permission दो — बस! 🎉
